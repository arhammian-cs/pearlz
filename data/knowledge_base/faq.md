# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is in the box?

The AquaPearl 500 Pro ships with: the purification unit, one standard
replacement filter cartridge (pre-installed), an inlet hose, an outlet
hose, plumber's tape, a quick-start card, and a power adapter. See the
Product Manual for the full parts diagram.

## How much does a replacement filter cost?

Pearlz sells two filter cartridge options for the AquaPearl 500 Pro: the
Standard Replacement Filter and the Pro Replacement Filter, which last
longer and are rated for higher sediment loads. Pricing for both options is
listed in the Pricing Guide -- check which cartridge you have installed
before ordering, since the two are priced differently.

## Do you offer student discounts?

No. As stated in the Refund & Returns Policy, Pearlz does not offer student
discounts, and Customer Support cannot apply discount codes that are not
part of an official Pearlz promotion.

## Can I install the unit myself?

Yes. The Installation Guide includes a certified self-install checklist.
Following it is required to remain eligible for Property Damage Coverage
under the Warranty Policy.

## What should I do if I smell or taste something unusual after replacing my filter?

This is expected immediately after a filter change. Follow the First-Run
Flushing instructions in the Installation Guide, then see the
Troubleshooting Guide if the issue does not resolve.

## What happens if my unit is more than 30 days old and I want to return it?

Standard returns are only available within the 30-day window described in
the Refund & Returns Policy. After 30 days, please refer to the Warranty
Policy for coverage of manufacturing defects.

## Is Pearlz Customer Support available by phone?

Please use the contact channel provided in your Pearlz account portal or
order confirmation email to reach Customer Support.
