# AquaPearl 500 Pro Troubleshooting Guide

## 1. Indicator Light and Beep Codes

| Signal | Meaning | What To Do |
|---|---|---|
| Solid green light | Normal operation | No action needed |
| Solid red light | Filter cartridge expired | Replace the filter cartridge per the Product Manual |
| Slow-flashing amber light | Filter due for replacement within 2 weeks | Order a replacement filter |
| Three beeps with flashing red light | Leak sensor triggered | Shut off the cold water supply valve immediately. Unplug the unit. Contact Pearlz Customer Support. Do not attempt to disassemble the unit yourself, as this may void your warranty. |
| No light at all | Unit not receiving power | Check the power adapter and outlet; contact support if the issue persists |

## 2. Water Tastes or Smells Unusual

If water tastes or smells unusual immediately after installing a new
filter cartridge, this is almost always residual carbon fines from the new
cartridge. Follow the First-Run Flushing steps in the Installation Guide
(run water for 2 minutes) before judging water quality. If unusual taste or
odor persists after a full 2-minute flush, the filter cartridge may be
seated incorrectly -- reseat the cartridge per the Product Manual and
flush again.

## 3. Reduced Water Flow

Reduced flow is most commonly caused by a clogged or expired filter
cartridge. Check the indicator light: a solid red light means the filter is
expired and should be replaced.

## 4. Unit Leaking

If you notice a leak that is NOT accompanied by the three-beep alarm,
shut off the water supply and inspect the fittings described in the
Installation Guide. If the leak recurs or has caused property damage,
follow the Property Damage Claim Process in the Warranty Policy.

## 5. When to Contact Support

Contact Pearlz Customer Support directly for: leak-sensor alarms, unusual
electrical noises, error conditions not listed in this guide, or any issue
that persists after following the steps above.
