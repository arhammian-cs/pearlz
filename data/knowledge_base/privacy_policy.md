# Pearlz Home Systems Privacy Policy

## 1. Information We Collect

When you register your AquaPearl unit or contact Customer Support, Pearlz
collects your name, contact details, order information, and installation
date. If you use the optional online warranty registration form, we also
collect the registration date used for Property Damage Coverage
eligibility.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process orders, provide warranty and support
services, and send service reminders such as filter replacement notices.
Pearlz does not sell customer data to third parties.

## 3. Data Sharing

Pearlz shares customer information only with shipping carriers (to deliver
orders) and payment processors (to process transactions). Service and
Maintenance Agreement customers may have installation and maintenance visit
data shared with the certified technician network that services their
account.

## 4. Data Retention

Order and warranty records are retained for the duration of the warranty
period described in the Warranty Policy, plus 3 additional years for
recordkeeping purposes.

## 5. Your Rights

You may request a copy of the personal information Pearlz holds about you,
or request correction or deletion of that information, by contacting
Pearlz Customer Support. Deletion requests may be limited where records
must be retained to honor an active warranty or Service and Maintenance
Agreement.

## 6. Security

Pearlz uses industry-standard safeguards to protect customer data but
cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted online.
