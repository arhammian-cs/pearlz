# AquaPearl 500 Pro Installation Guide

## 1. Before You Begin

Confirm your box contents against the Product Manual before starting
installation. You will need an adjustable wrench, plumber's tape, and
access to your cold water supply line under the sink.

## 2. Certified Self-Install Checklist

To qualify for Property Damage Coverage under the Warranty Policy, a
self-installed unit must satisfy every item on this checklist:

1. Cold water supply line shut off before installation.
2. Inlet and outlet fittings wrapped with plumber's tape and hand-tightened,
   then given a quarter turn with a wrench.
3. Leak test performed by running water for 5 minutes and visually
   inspecting all fittings before returning the unit to normal use.
4. Installation date recorded on the warranty registration card or online
   registration form.

Units installed without following this checklist, or installed by anyone
other than the customer or a Pearlz Certified Technician, are not eligible
for Property Damage Coverage, even if the unit itself remains under
warranty.

## 3. Step-by-Step Installation

1. Turn off the cold water supply valve under the sink.
2. Connect the inlet hose from the supply valve to the AquaPearl 500 Pro
   inlet port.
3. Connect the outlet hose to your faucet or dedicated drinking-water
   spout.
4. Insert the included filter cartridge into the filter housing until it
   clicks into place.
5. Slowly turn the water supply back on and check all fittings for leaks.

## 4. First-Run Flushing

New units, and units that just received a new filter cartridge, must be
flushed before use: run water through the unit continuously for 2 minutes
to clear residual carbon fines from the new cartridge. Water may appear
slightly cloudy or taste different than usual during this flush -- this is
expected and resolves after the 2-minute flush. See the Troubleshooting
Guide if unusual taste persists after flushing.

## 5. Registering Your Unit

Register your installation date online or on the included card. This
registration date is used to confirm eligibility for Property Damage
Coverage claims described in the Warranty Policy.
