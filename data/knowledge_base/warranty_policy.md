# Pearlz Home Systems Warranty Policy

## 1. Overview

This warranty policy applies to all AquaPearl-branded water purification
products sold directly by Pearlz Home Systems or through authorized
resellers.

## 2. Warranty Duration

The AquaPearl 500 Pro is covered by a **24-month (2-year) limited warranty**
from the original date of purchase, covering manufacturing defects in parts
and labor for repair or replacement.

Replacement filter cartridges are not covered by the unit warranty and carry
no separate warranty beyond the standard 30-day return window described in
the Refund & Returns Policy.

## 3. What Is Covered

- Manufacturing defects in the purification unit housing, pump, and
  electronic control board.
- Parts and labor required to repair a covered defect.
- Property Damage Coverage: if a covered manufacturing defect causes a leak
  that damages surrounding property (for example, cabinetry or flooring),
  Pearlz will reimburse verified repair costs up to $500 per incident,
  **provided the unit was installed according to the Pearlz Installation
  Guide by a Pearlz Certified Technician or a self-installer who followed
  the Installation Guide's certified self-install checklist.**

## 4. Property Damage Claim Process

To file a Property Damage Coverage claim:

1. Shut off the water supply to the unit immediately.
2. Contact Pearlz Customer Support within 7 days of discovering the damage.
3. Provide proof of purchase, installation date, and photos of the damage.
4. Support will confirm whether the installation met the certified
   self-install checklist in the Installation Guide before approving
   reimbursement.

## 5. Exclusions

Warranty coverage, including Property Damage Coverage, is void if any of the
following apply:

- The unit was repaired, opened, or modified by anyone other than a Pearlz
  Certified Technician.
- The defect resulted from misuse, improper installation not following the
  Installation Guide, or use of non-Pearlz replacement filters.
- Normal wear items such as filter cartridges, gaskets, and seals.
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function.

## 6. How to Make a Warranty Claim

Contact Pearlz Customer Support with your proof of purchase and a
description of the issue. Do not attempt unauthorized repairs, as this will
void the warranty described in Section 5.
