Pearlz Home Systems Shipping Policy

Shipping Regions

Pearlz currently ships AquaPearl products only within the continental
United States. We do not currently offer international shipping, shipping
to Alaska or Hawaii, or shipping to PO boxes.

Shipping Methods and Timelines

Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days, included free on all AquaPearl 500
Pro orders.

Express Shipping: 2-3 business days, available for an additional fee shown
in the Pricing Guide.

Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1 business day of purchase. You will receive a
tracking number by email once your order ships. Delivery timelines begin
from the ship date, not the order date.

Damaged Shipments

If your AquaPearl unit arrives visibly damaged in transit, contact Pearlz
Customer Support within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the packaging
and product. Damaged-in-transit claims are handled separately from the
standard Warranty Policy and Refund & Returns Policy.

Order Tracking Issues

If your tracking number shows no movement for more than 5 business days,
contact Pearlz Customer Support so we can investigate with the carrier.
